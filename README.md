🚀 Excited to share a milestone in my AI journey!

My research paper, **"Prompt-Driven Tool-Calling for Lightweight Open Source LLMs,"** has been officially published in the proceedings of the **3rd International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Sustainable Computing for Smart Cities (AIS2C2 2025)**.

📚 **Publication Details**
• Author: Pawan Yadav
• Conference: AIS2C2 2025
• Pages: 493–497
• ISBN: 978-93-94086-43-2

🔍 **What problem does this research address?**

As Large Language Models become more capable, one major challenge remains: **how can smaller, open-source LLMs perform complex tasks without requiring massive computational resources?**

This research explores a **prompt-driven tool-calling framework** that enables lightweight open-source LLMs to:
✅ Use external tools effectively
✅ Perform multi-step reasoning workflows
✅ Reduce dependence on large proprietary models
✅ Move us closer to practical, cost-efficient AI agents that can run in resource-constrained environments

I strongly believe that the future of AI is not just about building bigger models—it's about making intelligence **more accessible, efficient, and deployable in the real world**.
